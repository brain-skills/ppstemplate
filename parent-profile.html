    Open House Day at Philadelphia Progressive School
    December 25, 2025

    We are excited to invite parents and prospective students to our Open House Day on September 15, 2024! This is a wonderful opportunity to tour our campus, meet teachers, and learn more about our innovative curriculum and extracurricular activities. We look forward to welcoming you and answering all your questions to help you get to know our school community.

    Enrollment for the 2025–2026 School Year is Now Open!
    December 25, 2025

    Philadelphia Progressive School is now accepting applications for new students for the upcoming academic year. Don’t miss the chance to join our vibrant learning community dedicated to academic excellence and personal growth. Apply early to secure your child’s place and start an exciting educational journey with us!

    Update Your School Passes Starting January 1, 2026
    November 7, 2025

    Attention students and parents! Please be reminded that starting January 1, 2025, all school passes must be updated to ensure continued access to school facilities and transport services. Visit the school office starting December 15 to renew or replace your pass without any hassle. Thank you for your cooperation!

    Important: School Bus Route #12 Changes
    October 22, 2025

    Please note that effective October 1, 2024, the route of school bus number 12 will be modified to improve service and reduce travel time. Updated route maps and schedules are available at the school office and on our website. We encourage all affected families to review the changes and plan accordingly.

    School Stadium Under Renovation During Holidays
    October 10, 2025

    We inform students and parents that the school stadium will be closed for renovation throughout the upcoming holiday period from December 20, 2024, to January 5, 2025. All sports practices scheduled during this time will be held at the city stadium. Please ensure students are aware of the temporary change in venue.
