    Weekend School Trip to the Zoo: Exploring Nature Together

    The school trip to the zoo is an exciting journey into the world of wildlife, where children will have the chance to see a variety of animals, including lions, giraffes, monkeys, and rare birds. The zoo features spacious enclosures, beautiful lakes, and themed areas that allow students to immerse themselves in different natural ecosystems and learn about the lives of animals in their natural habitats.
