Margaret Ellis

Literature

Distinguished Literature teacher with over 15 years of experience, holder of a Master's degree in English Literature, and recipient of the Philadelphia Educator Excellence Award.

Margaret Ellis is a highly experienced literature teacher at Philadelphia Progressive School with a strong record of inspiring students through storytelling, critical analysis, and modern curriculum methods.

She encourages creativity, thoughtful discussion, and analytical thinking. Beyond the classroom, Margaret supports curriculum development and student success initiatives across the school community.
