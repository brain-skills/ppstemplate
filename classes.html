English Language Curriculum for 5th Grade
Jane Henderson
English language
1. Parts of Speech

Introduction to nouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs, and pronouns. Students learn to identify and use these parts of speech correctly.

Academic hours: 12
Lessons: 12
Homework assignments: 10
Additional resources: 6
2. Sentence Structure

Understanding simple, compound, and complex sentences. Practice constructing various sentence types to improve writing clarity.

Academic hours: 18
Lessons: 15
Homework assignments: 12
Additional resources: 7
3. Vocabulary Development

Learning new words through context, synonyms, antonyms, and word families to expand active vocabulary.

Academic hours: 4
Lessons: 4
Homework assignments: 3
Additional resources: 12
Restricted content

The content of lessons, tests, homework, and other sections of the curriculum is available to registered users — students, teachers, and parents of students taking this course.

Grade 2: Reading Fluency

Emphasis on reading comprehension and double-digit addition.

Grade 3: Introduction to Fractions

Learning multiplication tables and basic essay writing.

Grade 4: Regional History

Focuses on state geography and long division.

Grade 5: Middle School Prep

Analyzing complex texts and introductory geometry.

Grade 6: Earth Science

Transitioning to multiple teachers and ratio-based math.

Grade 7: Life Science

Focus on biology, cells, and pre-algebra concepts.

Grade 8: Algebra Readiness

Final year of middle school; focus on physical science and civics.

Grade 9: Freshman Year

High school begins. Credits for graduation start accumulating now.

Grade 10: Sophomore Year

Focus on Geometry/Algebra 2 and preparing for college entrance exams.
